# Solution Architect - SAP Business AI Platform Exam Guide: Format & Study Plan

If you have spent years designing SAP landscapes, you probably expect certification exams to look a certain way: a timer, multiple-choice questions, and a score at the end. The C_BAIPA exam works differently. You talk through, or record, how you would solve a real architecture problem.

That is why a good [Solution Architect - SAP Business AI Platform exam guide](https://theexamquestions.com/blog/c-baipa-sap-exam-guide) matters. Memorizing facts will not carry you here. You have to explain a design decision clearly, under time pressure, to someone who is judging your reasoning.

This guide covers what the exam includes, how the assessment works, a six-week preparation plan, and the mistakes candidates commonly make. It is based on SAP's published certification details, so treat the official page as final on anything that may have changed.


## What This Certification Validates
The credential is called SAP Certified - Solution Architect - SAP Business AI Platform, and the exam code is C_BAIPA_2610.
According to the certification page on SAP Learning, it is meant for seasoned professionals who bridge business strategy and technology execution. A certified person should be able to turn complicated business requirements into a complete solution blueprint, and the page describes the role as designing and orchestrating those solutions. It is not a product-feature quiz. It sits closer to the work an enterprise architect does in a stakeholder workshop than to a configuration consultant's work in a system.


## Who It Is For
The role tags on the official page list four profiles:

•	Solution architects, who turn requirements into blueprints

•	Business architects, who connect strategy with technology choices

•	Applications architects, who decide how applications fit into the wider enterprise

•	Cloud security architects, who weigh risk in cloud-based designs

If you are early in your SAP career, you can still study for it, but expect the scenarios to feel abstract. Architecture judgment grows out of project experience, and no course fully replaces that.


## Exam Format At A Glance
These are the headline details from SAP's certification page:

•	Exam code: C_BAIPA (current version C_BAIPA_2610)

•	Assessment type: scenario-based, 1 activity

•	Duration: 2 hours

•	Pass score: 60%

•	Learning journey: 2 courses, advanced level

•	Languages: vary by format (see below)



## How The Open Book Format Works
SAP's practical assessments are open book. You can consult resources such as the SAP Help Portal, SAP Learning and SAP Community while you work.

Two hours go quickly if you are reading documentation for the first time during the exam. Open book access helps you confirm a detail, but it cannot supply the reasoning you are being asked to demonstrate.

The assessment grades the meaning of your answer and does not search for keywords. A well-structured explanation of why you chose an approach will beat a string of correct-sounding terms.


## Choosing Between AI-Guided Roleplay And Recorded Video
You can complete the assessment in one of two ways:

1.	Roleplay with AI guidance: You go through a virtual interview and answer scenario questions out loud. Your responses are recorded and assessed. This format is available in English, Spanish, Italian, French, Japanese, and many more languages.
   
2.	Recorded video: You follow the instructions in the assessment and submit a recording for review. This option is English only.

Pick the one that suits how you think. Some people reason better in a back-and-forth conversation, because the questions keep them on track. Others prefer a monologue at their own pace. Either way, you need a working microphone, and the video option may need camera access too. SAP also provides a Technical Readiness Checklist and an AI-powered practice tool, and both are worth using before exam day.


## Key Topic Areas
SAP groups the certification around integration strategies, technical architecture, application design, and solution governance. Business architecture, applications architecture, and cloud security architecture are listed as related skills.

•	**Integration strategies:** This covers how SAP Business AI Platform solutions connect to the wider SAP ecosystem and to non-SAP systems. Be ready to explain why you would choose one integration pattern over another, and what each choice costs in complexity, maintenance, and risk.

•	**Technical architecture:** Think resilience, scalability and sustainability. When a scenario describes growth, peak load, or a demanding availability requirement, you should be able to say what that implies for the design, beyond naming a service.

•	**Application design:** Applications have to satisfy business requirements and still fit the overall architecture. A common trap is designing something that solves the stated problem but quietly breaks a standard the organization depends on.

•	**Solution governance:** Many technical people undervalue this topic. It covers how you keep a solution aligned with architectural principles over time and how enterprise solutions are managed once they are live. Expect questions about who decides what, and why.

•	**Business and applications architecture:** Business architecture links strategy and requirements to technology choices. Applications architecture places each capability inside the enterprise blueprint. Together they test whether you can step back from a single project and see the whole picture.

•	**Cloud security architecture:** In a scenario, a sensible answer usually builds security into the design from the start and does not mention it as an afterthought.


## The Official Learning Journey
SAP recommends the [learning journey](https://learning.sap.com/learning-journeys/becoming-an-sap-business-ai-platform-solution-architect) called Becoming an SAP Business AI Platform Solution Architect. It currently has two courses:

•	**Exploring SAP Business AI Platform Architecture (about 3 hours 42 minutes, 6 units)**: It covers the strategic relevance of the platform, its pillars (Build, Contextualize and Reason, Govern), and how they support enterprise AI. Topics include creating AI agents and applications with Joule Studio, deploying them to the Joule Studio runtime, observability with SAP AI Agent Hub, and checking value realization with SAP Signavio Process Intelligence.

•	**Introducing the Architectural Methodologies (about 3 hours, 3 units)**: It introduces the core SAP methodologies that guide application extension, data and analytics, and integration initiatives.
The two courses total under seven hours, but preparation takes much longer. Practicing how to explain the material is the harder part.


## Six-Week Study Plan
This schedule assumes five to seven hours a week. Stretch it to eight weeks if your calendar is tight.

### Week 1: The role and the platform:
Read the certification page, then start the first course. Your goal is to describe the three platform pillars in your own words without notes. If you can't, repeat the unit.

### Week 2: Integration strategies:
Work through how the platform connects to other SAP and non-SAP systems. For every pattern you learn, write one sentence about when you would not use it. That habit trains the trade-off thinking the exam rewards.

### Week 3: Technical architecture:
Focus on resilience, scalability, and sustainability. Sketch two or three end-to-end designs on paper and explain them out loud, as if to a client.

### Week 4: Application design:
Study how applications meet requirements while staying consistent with the larger architecture. Finish the architectural methodologies course this week, since it gives you a vocabulary for justifying decisions.

### Week 5: Governance, business, applications and security architecture:
These wider topics are easy to skim, so give them real time. Take a sample scenario and ask four questions: Who owns this decision? Does it fit the strategy? Where does it sit in the blueprint? What are the security implications?

### Week 6: Rehearsal:
Run timed mock sessions. Speak your answers aloud, record yourself, and listen back. Check your microphone and technical setup, and review the open resources you plan to use so you can find things fast. Rest the day before.


## Common Mistakes

•	**Treating the exam as a recall test**: The assessment rewards reasoning, and an answer that lists services without explaining the trade-offs behind them will feel thin.

•	**Leaning on open-book access**: Looking things up eats the clock. Know the main ideas cold and use documentation only to confirm specifics.

•	**Skipping governance**: It feels less technical, so it gets the least revision, yet it is a named area of the certification.

•	**Never practicing out loud**: Explaining a design in your head is easy. Explaining it clearly to a microphone within two hours is a different skill.

•	**Ignoring logistics**: Technical readiness, attempt availability, and language options can all be sorted out in advance, so a setup problem should not cost you an attempt.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How long is the C_BAIPA exam, and what is the pass score?
The assessment lasts 2 hours, and the listed pass score is 60%. It is a single scenario-based activity with no multiple-choice questions.

### Is the exam really open book?
Yes. SAP's practical assessments allow you to consult resources such as the SAP Help Portal, SAP Learning, and SAP Community. You still have to apply that knowledge to a scenario within the time limit.

### Who should use a Solution Architect - SAP Business AI Platform exam guide like this one?
Solution, business, applications, and cloud security architects who already have project experience and want a structured way to prepare for a scenario-based assessment. Newer consultants can use it too, but should plan extra time to build architectural judgment.

### Can I take the assessment in a language other than English?
It depends on the format. The AI-guided roleplay is offered in English, Spanish, Italian, French, Japanese, and more. The recorded video option is English only.

### What happens if I fail an attempt?
You can generally retake the exam, subject to your available attempts and SAP's waiting rules. If you still have time left on the clock after an unsuccessful attempt, you may be able to continue immediately, and SAP treats that as one attempt. Check the exam page for the current policy.

### How do I keep the certification valid?
Certifications run on a 12-month cycle. To keep yours active, pass the relevant Stay Certified assessment before the expiry date. Access to those assessments is typically included with a SAP Learning Hub subscription.
