# C_THR83 Exam Official Details: What SAP Recruiter Experience Candidates Should Know
When searching for the [C_THR83 exam official details](https://theexamquestions.com/blog/c-thr83-sap-exam-guide), you have probably run into contradictory information. One says 80 multiple-choice questions in 180 minutes. Another describes a scenario-based assessment. A third quotes a passing score that matches neither. SAP changed how this certification is delivered, and many older articles were never updated.

This guide collects what SAP currently publishes about the SAP SuccessFactors Recruiting: Recruiter Experience certification, explains what the numbers mean in practice, and suggests how to prepare without spending weeks on outdated material. SAP updates exam versions regularly, so treat any third-party summary, including this one, as a starting point and confirm the details on SAP's own site before you book.

## C_THR83 Exam Official Details At A Glance
These details come from SAP's listing for the current exam version, C_THR83_2605.

•	Certification name: SAP Certified - SAP SuccessFactors Recruiting: Recruiter Experience

•	Exam code: C_THR83 (current version label: C_THR83_2605)

•	Format: One activity, scenario-based assessment

•	Duration: 120 minutes

•	Cut score: 60%

•	Intended role: Consultant

•	Learning journey: Configuring SAP SuccessFactors Recruiting: Recruiter Experience (intermediate level)

•	Journey content: 2 courses and 2 practical exercises

•	Validity: 12 months, extendable by passing the required assessment

The code and the version are different things. "C_THR83" is the certification family, and the suffix (such as 2605) identifies the exam release. That's why older pages mention labels like 2311 or 2505 and quote different numbers.
SAP has also indicated that a newer version is expected around mid-December 2026. If you plan to test in the coming weeks, check the SAP Learning page for the version currently open for booking.

## What The C_THR83 Certification Validates
The SAP Certified - SAP SuccessFactors Recruiting: Recruiter Experience certification is for people who configure and implement the recruiter-facing side of SAP SuccessFactors Recruiting. It tests configuration decisions: setting up job requisitions, shaping candidate profiles, applying permissions correctly, and understanding how the underlying platform supports those choices.

This affects how you study. Reading about features won't be enough, because you need to explain why you would configure something a certain way in a given business situation.

### Why the format changed
Older versions were multiple-choice and multiple-select, with a longer time limit. The current version is a single scenario-based assessment. You work through a realistic situation and show your reasoning, so it rewards people who understand the context: who the users are, what the business needs, and which configuration path fits.

### What open-book means
SAP describes its practical certification exams as open-book and non-proctored. You may use publicly available SAP resources, such as SAP Learning and SAP Help, during the assessment.

That sounds generous, but with 120 minutes on the clock, you won't have time to read documentation from scratch. People who do well already know where answers live and can find them in seconds. Two habits help:

•	Bookmark the SAP Help pages you use most while studying.

•	Practice answering scenario prompts out loud so your reasoning is clear when it counts.

### Delivery options and language
SAP's certification listing indicates that the scenario can be delivered in more than one way, such as an AI-guided role-play or a recorded video response, and that language availability differs by route. Check the options shown at booking so you know which one you're choosing and whether your preferred language is supported.
The exam timer keeps running even if you pause a role-play interaction, so plan your time accordingly.

## Topic Areas To Focus On
SAP's learning journey outlines the knowledge areas behind the assessment. Here is how I would group them and spread your effort.

### Platform foundations
Start with role-based permissions, People Profile, picklists, MDF objects, user data, and Configuration Center. They aren't exciting, but recruiting scenarios build directly on them. If your platform basics are shaky, every later topic takes longer.

### Job requisition configuration
This is the most technical part of the material. Expect to work with the standard job requisition, the job requisition data model, field permissions, recruiting groups and operators, team recruiting, and evergreen requisitions. If you can only go deep on one area, make it this one. Gaps show up quickly here.

### Candidate profiles
Candidate profile templates and their configuration determine what data you capture and how it's used throughout the hiring process. Getting the structure right early saves rework in a real project.

### Permissions and system settings
Proxy management, company system settings, and platform feature settings take little study time. They still change the correct answer in many scenarios because access and visibility decide what a user can actually do.

## The Official Learning Journey
SAP pairs the certification with a [learning journey](https://learning.sap.com/learning-journeys/configure-sap-successfactors-recruiting-recruiter-experience) called Configuring SAP SuccessFactors Recruiting: Recruiter Experience. It has four items:

1.	THR80: SAP SuccessFactors Platform Introduction Academy. It covers the platform foundation, and SAP lists roughly 8 hours and 43 minutes of content.
  
2.	Practical exercise one. Hands-on practice for the platform academy, in a practice system you unlock by registering.
   
3.	THR83: SAP SuccessFactors Recruiting: Recruiter Experience Academy. This is the core recruiting course, listed at roughly 16 hours and 54 minutes.
   
4.	Practical exercise two. A recruiting-focused practice system for applying what you learned.

SAP states that completing the whole journey isn't mandatory before taking a practical exam, but it strongly recommends it. In my view, skipping the hands-on exercises is the shortcut that most often backfires, because scenario questions test applied judgment more than recall.

## A Six-Week Study Plan
Here is a schedule that follows the structure above.
•	**Weeks 1 and 2**: Work through THR80 and the first practical exercise. Write short notes on permissions, MDF, and picklists in your own words.

•	**Week 3**: Start THR83. Focus on implementation planning, instance preparation, and recruiting admin setup, and sketch how setup choices affect later workflows.

•	**Week 4**: Spend the whole week on job requisitions and the data model. Reproduce configurations in the practice system instead of only reading about them.

•	**Week 5**: Cover candidate profiles, then revisit weak spots from earlier weeks.

•	**Week 6**: Run timed scenario drills. Do at least one full 120-minute rehearsal so pacing isn't a surprise.

If you already work in SuccessFactors, you can compress this. If you're new to the platform, give the early weeks more room.

## Registration and Scheduling Basics
Account problems can derail the process at the last minute, so a typical path looks like this:

1.	Create an SAP Universal ID, which certification activities require.
   
2.	Choose an access route. SAP offers options ranging from a single attempt to multi-attempt bundles and SAP Learning Hub subscriptions.
   
3.	Start from the certification page or from the learning journey once you have a valid attempt.
   
4.	Make sure the SAP user you test with matches the one tied to your subscription, so results and attempts are tracked properly.
   
5.	After passing, find your digital credential through Credly and your record under My Certifications.

Sort out the account side before your final revision week.

## Retake Rules and Validity
Under SAP's scenario-based policy, you can attempt the same certification up to four times within a 12-month period, with a 24-hour wait between attempts. If you use all four without passing, you must wait a full year from the last attempt.
If you fail with time still on the clock, you can retake right away, but it counts as an attempt. Review what went wrong first. Was it topic knowledge, scenario reasoning, or time management? Each needs a different fix.

Validity runs on a 12-month cycle, and passing the required assessment extends it by another 12 months. SAP Learning Hub subscribers can use shorter stay-certified assessments, while others can retake the full exam.

## Common Mistakes

•	Trusting outdated pages. Question counts, passing scores, and durations from earlier versions don't apply to the current format.

•	Assuming open-book is easy. Knowing where information is doesn't help if finding it takes five minutes.

•	Skipping hands-on practice. Reading configuration steps isn't the same as performing them.

•	Ignoring provisioning rights. SAP treats certification and provisioning rights separately. Provisioning rights go only to registered SAP partner consultants, so customers and independent consultants shouldn't assume certification unlocks system access.
